Hidden Phrase Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

Hidden Phrase ("the app") is a word puzzle game for Android made by Nathanael Berkeley ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

In short

  • You don't need an account. The app never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your puzzle progress is saved on your device. It is not sent to us.
  • The app offers optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob. Google's ad software collects some device information to show, measure and protect those ads, as described below.

Information saved on your device

The app saves your puzzle progress (boards, completed puzzles, the puzzle to continue), hints, earned and placed room decorations, and your settings (key-tap vibration, reduced celebration motion) in the app's private storage on your device. It also remembers which rewarded-ad letter is owed to which puzzle until the reward is delivered.

  • This information is not sent to us, and we have no server that receives it.
  • The app turns off Android cloud backup, so it isn't copied to your Google account backup.
  • On Android 12 and later, if you use Android's device-to-device transfer to set up a new phone, your puzzle progress can move to the new phone. Ad and consent data does not move.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes this information. We cannot recover it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK, provided by Google, to offer rewarded ads: you can choose to watch an ad to reveal one letter. There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play. To have an ad ready when you choose one, the app loads ads in the background once Google’s consent tool indicates that ad requests are permitted. This means Google's SDK may collect data even if you never watch an ad.

According to Google, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares the following data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention purposes:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of your device.
  • Interactions with the app and ads, such as app launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostic information about the performance of the app and the SDK, such as launch time, hang rate and energy use.
  • Device identifiers: the Android advertising ID, the app set ID and, where applicable, other identifiers related to signed-in accounts.

Google uses this information for advertising (including, where permitted, personalised ads), ad measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention and security. It is encrypted in transit. The SDK may also use Android's Privacy Sandbox advertising features where available on your device. We use AdMob reporting to understand advertising performance. We do not receive your individual advertising identifiers through the game or link advertising reports to your support emails or puzzle progress.

Google's handling of this data is described in:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Your choices

  • Ad privacy choices. Each time the app starts, Google's consent tool checks which privacy choices apply where you are. In US states whose privacy laws Google's tool supports, you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for advertising. Open the app's Settings and tap "Ad privacy choices"; the button appears when such choices are available for your location. The available options depend on your location and Google’s consent tool. Opting out does not necessarily stop all advertising data collection; information may still be used to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (for example Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on the device).
  • Not watching ads. The game can be played and finished without ever watching an ad.

Contacting support

If you email berkeleynathanael@gmail.com, we receive your email address, any name displayed with it, and the messages and attachments you send. We use this information to answer your question, troubleshoot problems and handle privacy requests. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information that is not needed for your request.

Support correspondence is processed through Gmail, provided by Google. We do not use support emails for advertising or add you to a marketing list.

Retention and deletion

  • Local game data: progress and settings remain on your device until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. Copies transferred to another device must be deleted on that device separately. We cannot remotely access, restore or delete your local progress.
  • Support emails: we delete support correspondence within 12 months after the issue is resolved, unless it is needed for an ongoing dispute or legal obligation. In those cases, we keep only what is necessary and delete it when that need ends.
  • Google’s advertising data: Google determines retention under its own policies. Clearing the app’s storage, uninstalling it or opting out does not automatically erase information already received by Google. See Google’s retention information and Google’s privacy controls.

To request access, correction or deletion of information we hold, email berkeleynathanael@gmail.com with the subject “Hidden Phrase privacy request”. We may ask for information needed to verify the request. We will respond as required by applicable law and explain any limitation. We cannot directly delete Google’s independently held advertising records; Google’s privacy links above explain its available controls.

Sharing a result

If you tap "Share result", Android's share menu opens with a short message such as "I solved Hidden Phrase level 12!". Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share with, and the message does not include the puzzle's phrase.

Children

Hidden Phrase is intended for people aged 13 and over and is designed primarily for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app itself doesn't ask anyone for personal information; see "Advertising" for what Google's ad software collects. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, contact us at berkeleynathanael@gmail.com so we can investigate and delete information we hold as appropriate.

Selling and sharing personal information

We do not sell your information for money. Information is processed by Google for advertising and by our email provider when you contact support, as described in this policy. We may also disclose information when required by law. Under some US state privacy laws, letting an advertising partner such as Google collect data for personalised ads can count as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. You can opt out as described in "Your choices" ("Ad privacy choices" in Settings, where available for your location).

Security

Information the app saves stays in Android's private app storage, protected by Android’s app-storage safeguards. Data sent by Google's ad SDK is encrypted in transit. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date. For material changes, we will also provide notice through the app or its Google Play release notes, and obtain consent where required.
