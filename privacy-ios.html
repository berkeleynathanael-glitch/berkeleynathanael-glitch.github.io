Hidden Phrase: Clover’s Corner for iPhone · Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Hidden Phrase: Clover’s Corner ("the app", shown on your Home Screen as "Hidden Phrase") is a word puzzle game for iPhone made by Nathanael Berkeley ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

In short

  • You don’t need an account. The app never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your puzzle progress is saved on your iPhone. It is not sent to us.
  • The app offers optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob. The app asks Google to show only non-personalized ads. Google’s ad software still collects some device information to show, measure and protect those ads, as described below.
  • The app does not track you across other companies’ apps and websites, and it never asks for permission to do so.

Information saved on your iPhone

The app saves your puzzle progress (boards, completed puzzles, the puzzle to continue), hints, earned and placed room decorations, and your settings (key-tap vibration, reduced celebration motion) in a file inside the app’s own storage on your iPhone. It also remembers which rewarded-ad letter is owed to which puzzle until the reward is delivered.

  • This information is not sent to us, and we have no server that receives it.
  • The progress file is excluded from iCloud Backup and from backups made with a computer, so it is not restored to a new or reset iPhone.
  • Deleting the app deletes this information, and we cannot recover it. (If iOS offloads the app to save space, iOS keeps the app’s data until you reinstall or delete it.)

Google’s software also stores some information in the app’s storage on your iPhone, including your ad privacy choices, so it can apply them each time the app starts. Unlike the progress file, this information can be included in your iPhone’s backups.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK, provided by Google, to offer rewarded ads: you can choose to watch an ad to reveal one letter. There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play. To have an ad ready when you choose one, the app loads ads in the background once Google’s consent tool indicates that ad requests are permitted. This means Google’s SDK may collect data even if you never watch an ad.

Ads are selected through Google’s ad platform and may come from many different advertisers. The app contains no other advertising or analytics software.

Non-personalized ads only. For every ad request, the app tells Google to turn off ads personalization, and it turns off Google’s publisher first-party ID (an identifier Google can otherwise assign to personalize ads within our apps). Google describes non-personalized ads as ads that are not based on your past behavior; they use contextual information such as general (for example city-level) location and the app you are using. Google says non-personalized ads still use identifiers for frequency capping (limiting how often you see the same ad) and aggregated ad reporting.

No tracking permission, no advertising identifier. The app never asks for permission to track you, so iOS does not give the app or Google your device’s Advertising Identifier (IDFA).

According to the privacy disclosures Google includes with its iOS software, the Google Mobile Ads SDK can collect:

  • Coarse location, such as a general location estimated from your IP address.
  • Device identifiers that iOS makes available to apps without tracking permission.
  • Advertising data, such as the ads you are shown.
  • Product interaction, such as app launches, taps and ad views.
  • Performance, crash and other diagnostic data about the app and the SDK.

Google’s consent tool (User Messaging Platform) uses coarse location, performance data and product interaction to work out which privacy choices apply to you and to show them.

Google uses this information to show and measure ads, for analytics, and for fraud prevention and security, as described in its own policies. Data sent by Google’s software is encrypted in transit. We use AdMob reporting to understand overall advertising performance. We do not receive identifiers for individual users through the game, and we do not link advertising reports to your support emails or puzzle progress.

The app also lists advertising networks for Apple’s SKAdNetwork, Apple’s privacy-preserving way to report whether an ad led to an app install without identifying you.

Google’s handling of this data is described in:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Your choices

  • Ad privacy choices. Each time the app starts, Google’s consent tool checks which privacy choices apply where you are. In US states whose privacy laws Google’s tool supports, you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information and of targeted advertising. Open the app’s Settings and tap "Ad privacy choices"; the button appears when such choices are available for your location. Your choice is saved on your iPhone and applied each time the app starts. Opting out does not necessarily stop all advertising data collection; information may still be used to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Tracking. The app never asks to track you. You can stop all apps from asking in the iPhone’s Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Not watching ads. The game can be played and finished without ever watching an ad.

Contacting support

If you email berkeleynathanael@gmail.com, we receive your email address, any name displayed with it, and the messages and attachments you send. We use this information to answer your question, troubleshoot problems and handle privacy requests. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information that is not needed for your request.

Support correspondence is processed through Gmail, provided by Google. We do not use support emails for advertising or add you to a marketing list.

Retention and deletion

  • Local game data: progress and settings stay on your iPhone until you delete the app. We cannot remotely access, restore or delete your local progress.
  • Support emails: we delete support emails, including any attachments, within 12 months after the support request is resolved, except where we are legally required to keep them longer. In that case, we keep only what the law requires and delete it when the requirement ends.
  • Google’s advertising data: Google determines retention under its own policies. Deleting the app or opting out does not automatically erase information Google has already received. See Google’s retention information and Google’s privacy controls.

To request access, correction or deletion of information we hold, email berkeleynathanael@gmail.com with the subject “Hidden Phrase privacy request”. We may ask for information needed to verify the request. We will respond as required by applicable law and explain any limitation. We cannot directly delete Google’s independently held advertising records; Google’s privacy links above explain its available controls.

Sharing a result

If you tap "Share result", the iPhone’s share sheet opens with a short message such as "I solved Hidden Phrase level 12! Can you uncover the mystery?". Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it, and the message does not include the puzzle’s phrase.

Children

Hidden Phrase is intended for people aged 13 and over and is designed primarily for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app itself doesn’t ask anyone for personal information; see "Advertising" for what Google’s ad software collects. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, contact us at berkeleynathanael@gmail.com so we can investigate and delete information we hold as appropriate.

Selling and sharing personal information

We do not sell your information for money. Information is processed by Google for advertising and by our email provider when you contact support, as described in this policy. We may also disclose information when required by law. Under some US state privacy laws, letting an advertising partner such as Google collect data for advertising can count as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. You can opt out as described in "Your choices" ("Ad privacy choices" in Settings, where available for your location).

Security

The information the app saves stays in the app’s own storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS’s app sandbox and data protection. Data sent by Google’s ad software is encrypted in transit. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

If the app’s data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date. For material changes, we will also provide notice through the app or its App Store release notes, and obtain consent where required.
