Hidden Phrase

Hidden Phrase is a calm word puzzle game by Nathanael Berkeley, for iPhone and Android.

  • iPhone: Hidden Phrase: Clover’s Corner
  • Android: Hidden Phrase

Support

For help, questions or privacy requests, email berkeleynathanael@gmail.com.

If you’re reporting a problem, please include:

  • your device model (for example, iPhone 13 Pro Max or Pixel 7);
  • the app version, shown in the app under Settings › About;
  • a description of what happened, and what you were doing when it happened.
