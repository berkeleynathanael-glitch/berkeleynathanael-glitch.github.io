Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Swiparoo has no accounts, and your progress isn't synced between devices. It's included in your device's iCloud or computer backup, depending on your backup settings. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Ads only appear if you tap Watch ad · Find 1 word, which shows a short video ad and then finds a word for you. You can play every puzzle without it.

How do I add the Home Screen widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Swiparoo. There's a small and a medium size. Tapping the widget opens your current puzzle.

How do I change my privacy choices?

Where privacy laws give you a choice about how your information is used for ads, a Privacy choices button appears on the Gardens screen, so you can review or change your choice at any time. See the privacy policy for details.

Can I play a puzzle again?

Yes. Open a garden and choose any puzzle you've already solved. Replaying doesn't change your progress.
